# The Don't Be Evil Company

Hello and welcome to the official GitHub profile for **The Don't Be Evil Company**!

We are an open-source organization dedicated to building free,
high-quality,
and community-driven tools.

Our mission is to empower developers with the resources they need to make their work and lives easier.

We believe in the power of open collaboration and giving back to the community.
All of our projects are and will always be free, as we are committed to providing valuable tools without barriers.

### 🤝 Join Our Community

We are always looking for new contributors to help us make the world a better place,
one project at a time.

Whether you're a seasoned developer or just starting your open-source journey, your contributions are welcome.

Feel free to open an issue, submit a pull request, or simply star a repository to show your support.

Thanks for stopping by!
